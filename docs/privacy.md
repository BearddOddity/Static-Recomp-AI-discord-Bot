# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This policy explains what the **Static Recomp AI Discord Bot** (the "Bot") collects, why, who sees it, and how long it is kept.

The Bot is run by **[OPERATOR NAME]**. Contact: **[CONTACT EMAIL OR DISCORD HANDLE]**.

## What we collect

- **Discord IDs and names:** your user ID and username, plus the IDs of the channels and messages involved.
- **Messages:** the text, links, and images the Bot can read in channels it can see. This is needed to answer questions and to moderate.
- **Recent chat:** when the Bot answers in a channel, it reads a few recent messages from that channel for context. The Server decides how many.
- **Moderation records:** case reports, warnings, appeals, moderator votes, and a copy of the rules text.
- **Help threads:** if the Server has help threads turned on, the thread and its messages.
- **Settings:** options saved from Discord, such as `/settings`, `/tune`, and `/ai-mode`.
- **Joins:** only if the Server has a welcome message turned on.

## Why we use it

- To answer `/ask`, mentions, replies, and questions in assistant channels.
- To find scams, piracy, and rule breaks, and to send warnings.
- To let moderators review and decide cases.
- To run appeals and help threads, where the Server has them turned on.

## Who it is shared with

- **AI providers:** message text, recent chat, and images are sent to the AI service the operator chose, for example OpenRouter, Anthropic, Groq, or Gemini. Each service has its own privacy policy. Some free models may use what they receive to improve their models. The operator can also run a local model, in which case nothing leaves their computer.
- **Discord:** the Bot posts replies, case reports, and direct messages through Discord.
- **GitHub:** the Bot downloads public documentation from GitHub. No member data is sent to GitHub.
- **Moderators:** they see case reports, including message text and images.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

- On the computer or server that runs the Bot, in its `data` folder: a database with cases, warnings, appeals, votes, help threads, and the rules copy, plus a few small settings files.
- Case reports are also posted in the Server's moderation log channel.
- Follow-up memory, used for follow-up questions, is kept in memory only and is cleared when the Bot restarts.

## How long we keep it

- Warnings stop counting after the number of days the Server sets (90 by default). The records stay until they are deleted.
- All other records stay until the operator deletes them. The Bot has no automatic deletion yet.
- Case reports in the moderation log stay until a moderator deletes them.

## Your choices

- To ask us to delete your records, contact us at the address above.
- Don't post passwords, account numbers, or other private details in channels the Bot can read.

## Security

- Keys and tokens are kept in a private settings file, never in public code.
- No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute safety.

## Age

- Discord requires users to be at least 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

- We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## Discord's policy

- Discord also handles your data under [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
